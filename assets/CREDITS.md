# Artwork

GothicVania Church by Luis Zuno (@ansimuz), CC0/public domain:
https://opengameart.org/content/gothicvania-church-pack
Architecture, tiles, burning ghoul, and skeleton wizard. Cropped and scaled for this game.

Castle Platformer by Jetrel, CC0/public domain:
https://opengameart.org/content/castle-platformer
Elisa knight, bats, and landscape. Background keys removed and animation frames cropped.

No commercial Castlevania assets are included. Art is embedded at build time;
run `python tools/build_assets.py` with Pillow to regenerate `source/art.inc`.
